# Loupe

## Language

### Lightweight playback

A mode in Loupe for playing, pausing, seeking and stepping through videos and animated images one frame at a time, with low resource use while browsing a folder. Opening the current video in the standalone FrameScrub app starts it paused at the position shown in Loupe.

### Scrub Lock

A temporary input mode for the current video or animation. It activates FrameScrub's playback gestures and transport shortcuts while the viewer stays in Loupe. Shift-Control toggles the mode and Escape exits it.

### Zoom carry

The rule that translates a free zoom from one photo or viewing area to another. It can preserve pixel scale, the visible share of the photo, or the share of the viewing area covered by the photo.

### Photo framing

The zoom and the part of the photo visible in the viewer. Position matters on an axis where the photo extends beyond the viewing area.

### Remembered photo framing

A photo's own zoom and position from its previous visit. When the viewing area changes shape, the framing adapts to the new area and keeps the same point of interest as close to the center as the photo's edges allow.

### Zoom carry reference

The last zoom the user set for browsing. It is the starting point for carrying zoom to an unvisited photo, even if an intervening photo needed a different automatic scale.

## Home sources

**Web Images**

A Home source for finding images on the web by text query and opening a selected result in Loupe’s viewer.
